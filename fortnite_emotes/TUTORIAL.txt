FORTNITE EMOTES FOR CS2 - HOW TO USE
====================================

79 Fortnite dances, 39 emotes with their music, and CS2's own celebration
animations, on a Fortnite-style emote wheel. Only you see and hear them.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- emotes.dll
- The Emote Pack (a zip with the dance model, the music and the icons)
- A DLL injector that works with CS2


SETUP
-----
1. Open your Documents folder and make a folder called:  Emotes

2. Extract the Emote Pack zip into Documents\Emotes.
   You don't need to tidy it up: the DLL looks up to 3 folders deep for
   the folder that has "csgo_addons" in it. It should look like this:

     Documents\Emotes\
       csgo_addons\characters\kolka\fortnite_dance.vmdl_c   (the dances)
       music\*.wav                                        (the songs)
       icons\*.png                                        (the wheel pictures)
       fonts\                                             (optional)

   Optional: put the "Burbank Big Condensed Black" font in the fonts folder
   (or install it in Windows) to get Fortnite's font on the wheel.

3. Start CS2 and inject emotes.dll into cs2.exe.
   The first time, it makes emotes.ini (your settings) and emotes.log
   in Documents\Emotes.

4. Join a match and press B.


CONTROLS
--------
B (tap)             Opens the wheel and keeps it open.
B (hold)            Opens the wheel; point at an emote and let go to play it.
Left click          Plays the emote under the mouse.
                    Clicking the round arrows under the wheel changes page.
Mouse wheel         Next / previous page.
Right click / Esc   Closes the wheel.
B (while dancing)   Stops the emote.
End                 Unloads the DLL.

Moving, shooting, dying or a new round also stops the emote.
The camera goes to third person while you emote, then back.

Wheel pages:
  FAVOURITES  your 6 picks from emotes.ini (page one)
  DANCES      the 79 Fortnite dances
  EMOTES      the 39 Fortnite emotes
  CS2         the game's own celebrations and poses (these have NO music)


SETTINGS (Documents\Emotes\emotes.ini)
--------------------------------------
Open it in Notepad, change a line, save, then press End and inject again.

  wheel_key=0x42     the wheel key (B), as a key code (see below)
  unload_key=0x23    unloads the DLL (End); 0 turns it off
  music=1            1 = dance music on, 0 = off
  volume=70          music volume, 0 to 100
  slot1=0 ... slot6=5
                     the Favourites page:
                       0 to 18    = a CS2 animation
                       1000 and up = a Fortnite dance or emote (list below)
                       -1          = empty slot

Key codes: a letter is its capital letter in hex
  A=0x41 B=0x42 C=0x43 ... G=0x47 H=0x48 ... T=0x54 V=0x56 X=0x58 Z=0x5A
  Mouse 4=0x05  Mouse 5=0x06  Middle mouse=0x04  Caps Lock=0x14
  Insert=0x2D  Home=0x24  End=0x23  F1=0x70 ... F12=0x7B


IF SOMETHING DOESN'T WORK
-------------------------
Check Documents\Emotes\emotes.log first. The same lines also show in the
CS2 console (~) starting with [emotes].

"Emote Pack not found"
  The pack isn't in Documents\Emotes, or it's more than 3 folders deep.
  Move the folder that has csgo_addons closer to the top.

Only the CS2 pages show on the wheel
  The dance model hasn't loaded yet. It loads a few seconds after you
  join a match. If it never does, check the log.

No music
  The Favourites page starts with CS2 animations, which never have
  music. Try something from the DANCES page. Check music=1 in
  emotes.ini, and that CS2 isn't muted in the Windows volume mixer.
  The log says "playing ....wav" each time a song starts.

The emote stops straight away
  Moving or shooting stops it. Stand still before you pick an emote.
